Physics

The physics section mainly focuses on mechanics and electricity for semester two. The content covers most high school physics, university physics, IB Physics (SL and HL), AP Physics 1, and AP Physics 2.

Because different curricula have different syllabi, some topics are organized differently. For example, electricity and magnetism in IB Physics includes electric potential, while other curricula may treat electric potential as an independent topic.

Most of the content is shared across introductory physics courses, although topics may be arranged in different orders. This website is designed primarily for AP, IB, high school, and introductory university physics students.
