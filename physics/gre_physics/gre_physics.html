GRE Physics

What is the Physics GRE?

The Physics GRE is a standardized subject test administered by ETS and used by some graduate programs in physics and related fields. The exam tests knowledge across a broad range of undergraduate physics topics and places significant emphasis on both conceptual understanding and problem-solving speed.

Helpful Book

Conquering the Physics GRE

Conquering the Physics GRE by Adam Anderson and Yoni Kahn is a comprehensive study guide that includes content review, practice problems, full-length tests, and test-taking strategies.

The book's concise format is useful for review, but students who need more foundational instruction in a particular subject may find some explanations too condensed. In those cases, standard undergraduate textbooks can be useful as supplementary resources.
